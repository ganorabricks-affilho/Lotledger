Each figure is checked on its own against the parts in Inventory. The number you can build is set by the scarcest part. One loose part can qualify more than one figure. New and used quantities are added together. The list runs from the highest profit. BrickLink price is the average paid for a used figure over the last 6 months. Parts listed is your price for one figure, after any sale, using the lowest price when a part is listed more than once. Profit is that figure price minus the parts. Check the figures you want to build. Review build keeps the set that fits in the parts you have and pays the most, then lists the drawers to pick from.
